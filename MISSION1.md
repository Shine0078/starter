## Additional ideas that would make this stand out

Beyond what you've already described, these features could differentiate the app from most personal finance tools:

* **Cash Flow Forecasting:** Predict account balances 7, 30, and 90 days into the future based on recurring income and expenses.
* **Bill Negotiation Assistant:** Highlight recurring services that may be negotiable and provide guidance or partner integrations where available.
* **Shared Expense Tracking:** Automatically split bills with friends or roommates and remind people of outstanding balances.
* **Merchant Price Intelligence:** Show when recurring services increase in price and estimate annual impact.
* **Net Worth Dashboard:** Combine bank accounts, investments, loans, and property values for a complete financial picture.
* **Emergency Fund Calculator:** Recommend an emergency fund target based on actual monthly expenses and track progress.
* **Financial Calendar:** Display paydays, bill due dates, subscriptions, and planned savings contributions in one calendar view.
* **Custom Automation Rules:** For example, "Every Uber Eats purchase goes into Food Delivery" or "Round every purchase up and allocate the difference to savings."
* **AI Spending Simulator:** Let users ask, "If I buy this $1,200 laptop, how will it affect my finances over the next three months?"
* **Financial Life Timeline:** Show milestones such as first $10,000 saved, debt-free date, largest monthly savings, and yearly spending trends.
* **Privacy Dashboard:** Clearly show what data is stored, where it is stored, what permissions are enabled, and allow users to revoke access at any time.
* **Regional Compliance Layer:** Adapt supported banks, currencies, taxes, and regulations automatically based on the user's country.

With a strong emphasis on privacy, intelligent automation, and actionable financial coaching—not just transaction tracking—this project has the potential to become a comprehensive financial management platform rather than simply another budgeting app.

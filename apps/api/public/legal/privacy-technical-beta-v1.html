FINVERSE technical-beta privacy notice

Version technical-beta-v1. This notice is for invited technical testers only. It is not a counsel-approved Privacy Notice and does not authorize a public consumer launch.

Account email, authentication metadata, and finance records you enter or connect are stored in FINVERSE PostgreSQL under row-level security. Bank access tokens are encrypted. Crash reports, if enabled, are redacted of tokens, emails, and long numbers.

Do not submit other people's data. You may export or delete your account from Settings. Replace this document with a counsel-reviewed Privacy Notice before a commercial launch.
